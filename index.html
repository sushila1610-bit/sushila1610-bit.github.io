PathLock

Arrow Escape

Tap an arrow and it zooms off the board, but only if its path is clear. Work out the right order, clear every arrow, and earn three stars.

Coming soon on Google Play

Features

  • 600 puzzles across 20 worlds, from gentle starters to expert brain-burners.
  • New twists: ice, keys and locks, mystery arrows, bombs, moving blocks, move limits and timed puzzles.
  • Daily Challenge: a fresh puzzle every day, with streak rewards.
  • Plays offline: no account needed.
  • Free to play: optional ads for hints and coins. No purchases, ever.
  • Undo, hints and trophies to help you master every board.

Download

PathLock is in testing and will be available on Google Play soon for Android phones.

Support

Found a bug, have an idea, or need help? Email polkiju.apps@gmail.com and include your phone model and the level number if it's about a puzzle.

To delete your game progress, use Settings → Reset Progress in the game, or uninstall the app. The game keeps your progress only on your phone.
