PathLock – Arrow Escape

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

Who we are

PathLock – Arrow Escape ("the game", "we") is a puzzle game for Android. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at polkiju.apps@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, stars, coin balance, daily-challenge streak, trophies, settings and gameplay statistics only on your phone. We do not run servers or user accounts, so this information is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or using Settings → Reset Progress deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • device and app information (such as phone model, Android version and app version);
  • ad interactions (such as views and taps) and diagnostic information.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are used. Where applicable, you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Watching rewarded ads is always optional. The game never sells coins, hints or anything else. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

App updates and app safety

The game uses Google Play's in-app update service to tell you when a new version is available. This check happens between your phone and Google Play, under Google's privacy policy; we receive nothing.

To warn you about unofficial, modified copies, the game checks on your phone which certificate signed the installed copy. This check never leaves your device.

Analytics

The game counts basic gameplay events (for example, a level started or completed) on your device to improve the game. These counts are not sent to us. If we add an analytics service in a future version, we will update this policy first to name it and describe what it collects.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad content in the game is limited to a general-audience (PG) rating.

Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell personal data. Information is shared only with Google (AdMob and Google Play) as described above, for serving and measuring ads, preventing fraud and delivering updates.

Security and retention

Saved game data stays on your phone until you delete it. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's policies. Connections made by the game use encryption (HTTPS).

Your choices and requests

You can reset your advertising ID, change your ad consent choice, reset your progress in the game, or uninstall the game at any time. For any privacy question or request, email polkiju.apps@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page, with its effective date at the top.
